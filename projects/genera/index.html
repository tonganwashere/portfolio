Genera

A flashcard study web app that works offline and is pre-loaded with trivia, allows you to create your own cards, and syncs everything across devices with account-based conflict resolution.

Python Flask JavaScript SQLite IndexedDB Service Worker

About

Genera is a flashcard study app I built to get better at general knowledge trivia. It comes loaded with 4,500+ questions across 24 categories pulled from Open Trivia DB, and you can also write your own cards or import them from a CSV or JSON file. The main study mechanic is a 5-box Leitner system: cards you get right move up a box, cards you get wrong drop back to box 1, and each box has a longer review interval. You work through cards due today, and the app tracks your progress over time.

The whole thing also works offline where a service worker caches the app shell so it loads without a connection, and all study data lives in IndexedDB on the device. If you create an account, your cards and progress sync to a Flask backend with a SQLite database on Render. The sync uses a dirty-flag and last-write-wins strategy per record, and there's a notification banner if a conflict is detected so you can choose which version to keep. You can also sign up anonymously and merge your data into a real account later without losing anything.

The frontend is vanilla JavaScript organized in an MVC structure with no framework. The backend is Flask with Gunicorn, and the database schema covers users, cards, progress, settings, and rate limiting.
