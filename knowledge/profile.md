## Personality & Engineering Philosophy
- **Anti-Overengineering & Radical Simplicity:**
  - Believes code should be as clean, lightweight, and simple as humanly possible.
  - Absolutely hates overengineering, convoluted code patterns that make no practical sense, or boilerplate code that doesn't need to exist.
  - Writes software with zero unnecessary framework bloat, preferring vanilla implementations whenever possible.
- **The Real Origin of his Offline-First Obsession:**
  - His commitment to building offline-first apps (*Genera*, *PyDFile*, *Bread & Blades*) comes directly from his real-world living situation: he lives in a modest rented room in Phnom Penh where the Wi-Fi barely works, forcing him to rely on his phone's mobile hotspot for nearly everything.
  - Experiencing firsthand how frustrating it is when basic digital tools stop working the moment connectivity drops, he designs his apps so users never need an active internet connection just to get things done.
- **Favorite Programming Language — Java:**
  - If he could only pick one language for the rest of his life, it would be **Java**.
  - Why Java: He finds it exceptionally versatile and honestly simpler and more consistent than other languages. Because it is strictly 100% Object-Oriented (instead of an optional, mixed paradigm like C++ or Python), everything follows a predictable structure. He also loves that Java's keywords and standard libraries read like clear, straightforward English.
- **Career Goals & Financial Pragmatism:**
  - Refreshingly candid and unpretentious: His primary motivation is earning good money through software engineering so he can achieve true financial security and never have to worry about money again.
  - Has no elitist bias toward specific niches or frameworks—as long as it's tech-related, he is eager to dive in.
  - Confident in his ability to deliver: knows that a professional job is fundamentally similar to building his own complex side projects, just scaled up with team collaboration, manager feedback, and real-world client pressure (which he welcomes).
