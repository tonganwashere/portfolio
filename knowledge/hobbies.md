# Hobbies, Interests & Personal Life

## 1. Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)
- **Main Hero — Julian (The Scarlet Raven):**
  - Julian is Tongan's favorite hero in all of MLBB.
  - **Playstyle:** Extreme aggression. Tongan loves Julian's insane burst damage because it fits his dive-heavy playstyle: chaining Skill 1 and Skill 2 to jump in headfirst—either getting the kill or dying trying!
- **Rank & Skill Level:**
  - **Peak Rank:** **Mythical Glory**.
  - **Current Standing:** Usually hangs out around **Mythic**; he plays more casually now that university, project development, and coding take priority.
- **Preferred Roles & Lanes:**
  - **Primary Role:** **EXP Lane** (loves the 1v1 brawler matchup).
  - **Secondary Role:** **Jungle** (for heavy map pressure and dive potential).
  - **Third Role:** **Mid Lane**.
- **Alternative Pocket Picks:**
  - When Julian is banned or picked, Tongan adapts with **Barats**, **Guinevere (Guin)**, **Gloo**, **Ruby**, or other sturdy brawlers depending on the enemy draft.
- **Queue Style:**
  - Mostly **solo queue**, but whenever friends invite him, he rarely turns them down because playing with friends is always the most fun and means losing a lot less!

## 2. Other Games & Gaming History
- **All-Time Most Played Game: Minecraft.**
  - Fell in love with it as a kid playing *Minecraft Pocket Edition (PE)* on mobile.
  - Fun childhood lore: Recorded and posted silly Minecraft PE gameplay videos on YouTube as a kid, reaching ~50 subscribers before deleting them years later.

## 3. Anime & Manga Taste
- **Viewing Preference:** Strongly prefers **watching anime** over reading manga because of the Japanese voice acting (he **strictly watches SUB**, never dub!) and the kinetic excitement of animated fight scenes and soundtrack.
- **Top Favorite Anime:**
  1. *Steins;Gate* (time travel, psychological tension, masterful pacing)
  2. *Vinland Saga* (character growth, philosophy of peace and redemption)
  3. *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood (FMAB)* (cohesive, unmatched storytelling)
  4. *Attack on Titan (AoT)* (scale, plot twists, tension)
  5. *Naruto* (classic shonen roots, emotional impact)
  6. *Pokémon* (a nostalgic favorite since early childhood)
- **Favorite Manga:**
  1. *Great Teacher Onizuka (GTO)*
  2. *All You Need Is Kill*
  3. *Saikyou Densetsu Kurosawa* (The Strongest Man Kurosawa / Kurosawa the Master) — a distinct, raw pick highlighting human resilience
  4. *Alice in Borderland* (high-stakes psychological survival games)
- **Characters He Admires & Aspires To:**
  - **Eikichi Onizuka (*GTO*):** Pure-hearted, deeply loyal, and kind to everyone, even if rough or unconventional.
  - **Thorfinn (*Vinland Saga*):** Emotional maturity, refusing to let pettiness or anger dictate his life, and embracing a peaceful outlook ("I have no enemies").
  - **Izuku Midoriya (Deku) & All Might (*My Hero Academia*):** Despite the anime/fandom being corny at times, he is deeply inspired by their pure, selfless goodness and drive to help others.

## 5. The Cats (Peanut & Milo)

- **Milo:** A 3-colored dark/calico female cat. Unlike Peanut, Milo is feisty, moody, and bites frequently (even Tongan and his sister sometimes!). She is, however, an exceptionally skilled hunter.

## 6. Stargazing & Outdoors
- **Stargazing:** Loved stargazing back in his hometown of Tboung Khmum, where the night skies are crystal clear without urban light pollution. Staring at the stars gives him immense peace and comfort. In Phnom Penh, the light pollution makes it impossible, so it's a cherished experience he misses.
- **Walks:** Enjoys taking solo walks to clear his mind, reset, and untangle complex architectural or coding challenges.
